# UK chocolate LightGBM with brand

The independent `lightgbm_with_brand` trainer implements native LightGBM
squared-error regression on log regular GBP per 100 g. It uses the frozen
supermarket experiment policy generated by `scripts/chocolate_experiment.py`.
The existing `chocolate-pricing-design-3` OLS trainer retains its own contract.
The six-model comparison, champion choice and release gates remain pending.

The working policy is unpublished. It requires explicit evidence for standard
single-pack supermarket bars, pack count one, reviewed physical/family/brand
identity, type, broad recipe and inclusion class, cocoa basis and optional claim
missingness. Unknown optional categorical evidence is explicit `unknown`;
`explicitly_absent` requires evidence. Allergen warnings cannot establish nut
ingredients. Numeric cocoa can be missing, with an explicit indicator and medians
learned by type/basis in fitting data, then the fitting-wide median as fallback.
Price and edible mass are never imputed. The supported initial retailers are
Ocado and Waitrose; Tesco requires its own evidence and validation.

```sh
  --gold-root data/gold/chocolate/uk/gold-4939405fcf8724686f9ee32c \
  --working-contract data/working-contracts/lightgbm_with_brand-v4/experiment.json \
  --price-window-start 2026-10-01T00:00:00+01:00 \
  --price-window-end 2026-10-03T23:59:59+01:00
```

The declared window is an attempted study configuration. It does not establish
freshness of unreviewed cached prices. Each eligible observation must have a
reviewed source observation time, positive regular tax-inclusive consumer pack
price and verified edible mass. The working study additionally requires explicit public
non-member, non-promotional single-pack regular-price context, online channel,
national online price scope and a source observation time basis. Missing context
blocks fitting rather than accepting a member or multibuy offer. The trainer checks the copied price observations,
normalized target, Gold managed hashes, typed logical row digests, Silver
provenance and contract copies. It consumes existing eligible rows and preserves
seller rows. Exit status 2 saves readiness blockers; status 1 indicates invalid
input or runtime; a fitted experimental run returns 0.

Runs are immutable under `data/models/chocolate/uk/lightgbm_with_brand/<run-id>/`.
Their identity includes model ID, exact input and contract hashes, frozen
experiment, implementation hashes, lockfile, package versions, preprocessing,
parameters, booster and predictions. `manifest.json` verifies every managed
artifact. `experiment.json` records cohort, price window, eligibility, missingness
and support policy, family/observation assignments and immutable data identities.
`reproduce.sh` saves the exact command. Readiness runs retain their evidence and
counts. Fitted runs add `booster.txt`, `model.json`, `preprocessing.json`,
`support-rules.json`, `calibration.json`, `evaluation.json` and predictions with
supported or rejected domain membership. `load_run` checks integrity before
returning a predictor with the same tree count, category maps, support and
retailer interval rules.

## Fitting, support and uncertainty

Families are ranked by SHA-256 of `1729:<family-id>`, with canonical ID as the
tie breaker. The first rounded 20% go to calibration, the next rounded 20% to
final testing and the remainder to fitting. There are no brand quotas. Three
validation folds rank fitting families by the same rule and assign rank modulo
three. Each partition or fold recomputes row weights as `1 / n_f`. Equal inputs
and policy reproduce equal assignments. Different snapshots produce separately
identified experiments and need common-row reconciliation before comparison.

Only fitting folds learn category codes, ranges, medians and admissible optional
terms. A reference-coded linear rank check enforces the shared known-brand
identification policy without fitting a comparator. Required core aliasing
blocks fitting; optional aliased or sparse terms are omitted and recorded. Numeric cocoa is also
omitted when its basis category is inadmissible. Identifiers,
source names, marketing text and price fields are outside the feature whitelist.

Bounded validation compares core and enriched features with two settings:
learning rate 0.03, 15 leaves/depth 4/L2 1, or 7 leaves/depth 3/L2 3.
Both start with minimum 20 rows per leaf, ceiling 2,000 iterations and patience
50. Validation uses family-weighted MAE after exponentiating unit predictions;
the lowest mean fold score selects the setting, with core and fewer leaves as
deterministic tie breakers. The median selected fold iteration count freezes the
final refit. Calibration and final testing cannot alter selection.

Category and brand/retailer/type context support require three fitting families.
Numeric inputs stay within frozen fitting ranges. Minimum leaf Hessian 3 works
with each family's total fitting weight one; leaf family counts are also saved
and enforced at prediction time. Three families are an engineering refusal
threshold, not evidence of release sufficiency. Native missing/category routing
cannot admit unseen brands, retailers, types or unsupported profiles.

Each retailer selects one calibration observation uniformly per family from a
sorted observation-ID list, using a seeded generator derived from seed 1729,
retailer and family. Selection is independent of targets and residuals.
Unsupported selected representatives are recorded and excluded without redraw.
The same rule applies to final testing. Unweighted absolute log residuals use
`ceil((n + 1) * 0.90)`; when that rank exceeds the count, the interval remains
unavailable/unbounded. Finite intervals exponentiate log limits and convert each
pack using its verified mass. The interval scope is representative families in
the declared retailer/snapshot population. New-brand, new-retailer and
future-price coverage remain unvalidated.

Evaluation reports family-weighted unit and pack MAE, weighted median absolute
percentage error, signed bias, listing-weighted sensitivity, support counts and
retailer/type/size/brand/cocoa-basis/missingness breakdowns. Strata below 30
families are flagged as sparse. Retailer strata recompute family weights.
Representative-family interval assessments report coverage, Wilson uncertainty
and median relative width. Comparator improvement gates and champion selection
remain pending. Retailer ablation and shared-versus-unique assortment diagnostics
remain separate outstanding comparison work.

## Explanations

The pinned LightGBM 4.6.0 native contribution path supplies exact raw-output
TreeSHAP using stored tree/path counts and no supplied background or
approximation. Every explained row checks reconstruction against an independent
raw prediction with absolute tolerance `1e-6` and relative tolerance `1e-5`.
Attributions use natural log GBP/100 g; exponential factors multiply, and currency
contrasts require complete supported predictions. The base is the model
explanation reference, with no claim that it is an observed market median or a
family-weighted explanation background. Global groups sum signed contributions
within each row before taking their magnitudes and averaging with family weights.

A deterministic narrative reports validated prices and explanation status.
Failed reconstruction suppresses every attribution claim while retaining the
validated prediction. Correlated brand/retailer/recipe/claim contributions describe
model associations. External AI provider integration is a subsequent task.
