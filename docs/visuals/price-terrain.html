How to read this landscape

A filled circle is the observed price. The open marker below or above it is the illustrative benchmark. The vertical stem is their difference. The violet diamond is your proposed price.

The surface holds undisplayed traits fixed. The all-trait fit view compares every product using its own full trait vector. Neither view establishes quality, demand or an optimal price.

No prediction interval is displayed: the hand-authored function has no calibrated uncertainty.
